Most apps are built to keep you scrolling. We wanted to build something that helps you put your phone down and actually be with people.

When we started designing Saha, the first thing we threw out was the algorithmic feed. No ranking, no engagement scoring, no "you might also like." Just a simple, chronological stream of moments shared by people you actually chose.

It sounds small, but it changes everything. Without an algorithm optimizing for attention, there's no incentive to post for an audience — only for the people who were actually there.
