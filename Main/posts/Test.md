No search bars, no cold requests. On Saha, you add people the same way you met them — in person.

Every account has a personal QR code. To connect, you scan each other's codes — that's it. No usernames to remember, no strangers sliding into anyone's profile.

It keeps your circle exactly what it should be: the people you actually know.
