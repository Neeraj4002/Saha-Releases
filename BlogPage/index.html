privacy by design

What you share
stays yours.

Saha was built with privacy as a foundation — not a feature. Every architectural decision was made to protect you, not monetize you.

Screenshots are disabled across the entire app — except your own QR page. Your moments stay in the moment.
No tracking pixels. No behavioral data sold. No ad profiles built from your activity.
Your friend graph is never analyzed or handed to third parties — ever.
No dark patterns. No infinite scroll. No streak mechanics designed to keep you hooked.
